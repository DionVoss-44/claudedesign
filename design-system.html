Design system

Scale, void and one violet.

The codified Cluster AI system: pure black canvas, weight-400 headlines that trust scale over weight, feather-light body copy, a single filled violet action and rationed amber sparks. Tokens live in design-system/.

Colors

Void — #000000

Page canvas and every section background. Pure black, never dark gray.

Bone White — #ffffff

Headlines, body text, nav active state.

Ash Gray — #9a9a9a

Muted nav text, ghost links, secondary labels.

Silver Mist — #bdbdbd

Tertiary body text and captions.

Electric Iris — #8052ff

Primary action buttons, logo mark, brand accents. The only saturated button color.

Saffron Spark — #ffb829

Uppercase section labels and inline emphasis. Rationed.

Deep Verdant — #15846e

Logo gradient stop and subtle accent washes only. Never a surface.
