# bar.md — Dala manifesto site (dala.craftedbygc.com/#manifesto)

Reference: "constellation floating on black velvet." Extracted from the Refero
extended teardown of the live site plus recovered page copy. Every line below is
checkable by looking at a render.

1. **The void is the design.** Every pixel of background is pure `#000000`.
   Zero cards, zero borders, zero shadows, zero dark-gray panels anywhere on the
   page. Elements are separated by whitespace alone.

2. **Scale does hierarchy, weight does not.** Headlines are weight 400 at
   78–113px with -0.04em tracking (visibly tight). Body copy is 18px at
   weight 200 — visibly airy, lighter than any normal site. No bold headlines
   exist anywhere.

3. **One violet, rationed amber.** Exactly one filled `#8052ff` pill button per
   viewport, maximum. `#ffb829` amber appears only as small uppercase section
   labels or an inline emphasis phrase — at most twice per screen. No other
   saturated color touches UI chrome. No gradients on any UI component.

4. **The hero art is a particle constellation, not an image.** Thousands of
   tiny outlined (1–2px stroke) triangles in vivid multicolor (violet, amber,
   teal, magenta, blue) form an organic brain shape, continuously drifting.
   Sparse ambient triangles float elsewhere on the page. No photography, no
   screenshots, no illustrations in the hero.

5. **One idea per viewport.** Each screenful holds a single headline block +
   supporting copy (+ at most one CTA). Two-column asymmetric rhythm that
   alternates sides section to section. Section gaps read as 60–120px+; the
   page never feels dense.

6. **The manifesto is a typographic monument.** A full-viewport statement set
   in display type (42px+) that progressively reveals as you scroll — words
   brighten from dim gray to white as they pass, with a small amber uppercase
   "Manifesto" label. It reads as the emotional core of the page, not a
   feature list.

7. **Motion is slow and resolves one way.** Content eases in over ≥400ms,
   always translating in a single consistent direction (up), never bouncing.
   Particles drift continuously and slowly. Nothing blinks or loops
   aggressively; a filmstrip of frames shows calm, monotonic motion.
